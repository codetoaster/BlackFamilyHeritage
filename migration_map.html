The Great Wagon Road

11 ocean crossings and 68 recorded movements within America, among 323 people. 23 of those movements begin and end inside the backcountry panel; a further 34 lead into or out of it and are drawn only on the map above. The Wagon Road is the historical route from Philadelphia through the Shenandoah Valley to the Carolina backcountry — the road the Blacks, Nortons and Brownlees came south on — and not any single journey. Living family members are not plotted, and places are approximate county or town centres, so a line shows that a family moved rather than the path they walked. The Connor line draws nothing here, which is itself the finding: every move recorded for them is between two places inside one county, so there is no distance to draw. 4 movements are not dated in the register and cannot be placed in the sequence, so they are left out of this panel rather than parked at an invented year.

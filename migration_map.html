The Crossing

Every line here is drawn from the family register: a movement recorded because a child was born somewhere a parent was not, or because someone died somewhere they had not been born. Thicker lines carry more people. Hover any line or place to read it.

Ocean crossing Movement overland The Great Wagon Road

11 ocean crossings and 74 overland movements among 323 people. The Wagon Road is drawn as the historical route from Philadelphia through the Shenandoah Valley to the Carolina backcountry — the road the Blacks, Nortons and Brownlees came south on — and not as any single journey. Living family members are not plotted. Places are approximate county or town centres, so a line shows that a family moved, not the path they walked.

The Crossing

Every line is drawn from the family register: a movement recorded because a child was born somewhere a parent was not, or because someone died somewhere they had not been born. Thicker lines carry more people. Hover any line or place to read it.

Ocean crossing Movement overland The Great Wagon Road

11 ocean crossings and 68 recorded movements within America, among 323 people. 23 of those movements begin and end inside the backcountry panel; a further 34 lead into or out of it and are drawn only on the map above. The Wagon Road is the historical route from Philadelphia through the Shenandoah Valley to the Carolina backcountry — the road the Blacks, Nortons and Brownlees came south on — and not any single journey. Living family members are not plotted, and places are approximate county or town centres, so a line shows that a family moved rather than the path they walked.

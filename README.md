# Black Family Heritage

Genealogy of the Black–Connor–Norton–Dillard–Adams–Burrell–Pruitt–Brownlee–Killingsworth–Salisbury families
of western North Carolina, upstate South Carolina, north Georgia, and colonial Virginia.

- **[The Black Family Register](index.html)** (`family_tree.html`) — interactive tree: fan chart, pedigree,
  223 people, 119 sources, ancestor photos, and embedded proof documents (wills, deeds, bonds, death
  certificates read from the original images).
- **[The Crossing](migration_map.html)** — the family's migration routes to and across America.

Every fact is graded (confirmed / probable / speculative) and cited. Living family members are redacted.
Compiled from census images, wills & estates, Revolutionary pensions, muster rolls, and lineage-society
records via the genealogy research database (system of record: private `family.db`).
